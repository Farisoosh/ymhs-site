01 THE MANIFESTO

Mental health isn't a topic.
It's the room we all live in.

01

Pause

One full day away from the noise. No lectures, no pressure, just ten hours to slow down and check in with your own mind.

02

Speak

Five committees, twenty-five conversations. From schizophrenia to bedtime procrastination, delegates say the quiet parts out loud, and find out they were never the only ones.

03

Heal

Not a conference you sit through, but a room you belong to. Every session ends the way it should, with people who understand a little more than they did that morning.

02 WHAT IS A COMMITTEE

Five rooms.

A committee is a small, guided circle. Each one is led by two chairs and built around five questions we rarely get to ask out loud. You pick the room that speaks to you, sit with your peers, and talk it through: a core condition, then four ideas that orbit it. No expertise required.
